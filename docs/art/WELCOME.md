# Welcome illustration

`public/art/welcome.png` was generated with the built-in imagegen tool for Pokémon Astra on November 17, 2026, without cartridge reference images.

Prompt: A 4:3 title-screen background intended for 320×240 logical pixels. Original island region at blue hour in authentic Game Boy Color era pixel art: teal and coral-roof village houses, jade trees, sandy path, wooden bridge and river, tiny warm windows, autumn forest, violet mountains, observatory on a hill, golden beacon, four-point star and restrained aurora. Lower two-thirds land, upper third clear dark teal sky for title overlay. Clean pixel clusters, limited palettes, no gradients, no antialiasing, no text, no logo, no Pokémon or characters, no interface. An upgraded 1999 handheld adventure aesthetic.

The original environment atlas is documented separately in `ENVIRONMENT-ATLAS.md`. Both assets are included in the production app and offline cache.
