# World content

`src/world/index.ts` is the source of truth for the region. It exports `WORLD`, `QUESTS`, `BADGES`, `ABILITIES`, and the corresponding types. Tile coordinates are integer cell positions. Towns are 32×28 or 40×32; routes are 40×24 or 28×40; dungeon and gym rooms are 28×24; interiors are 24×16. `#`, `r`, `w`, `~`, `B`, `C`, `P`, `T`, and `S` block movement (the latter five are interior furnishings); `.` is path, `g` is encounter grass, `d` is a doorway, `=` is a bridge, and `,` and `f` are decorative walkable terrain. Exits are bidirectional links; an exit's `requires` applies in that direction only. A transition should suppress retriggering until the player steps away from the destination exit.

Requirements combine all listed flags, minimum badge count, permanent field ability, caught count, owned species, type counts, and items. A successful switch or NPC interaction records its `setsFlag` and grants its reward once. Trainers and leaders award these only after victory. Legendary encounters remain retryable after a loss or escape, and record their flag only on capture. The starter interaction is special: it offers Growlithe, Poliwag, or Bellsprout and grants the initial supplies.

The central campaign uses the community objective before each gym, ordered gym switches before the keeper, the Bell Tower revelation before Meridian HQ, Vale's defeat before the observatory, the recovered lens before Orion, and all eight badges before stopping the Engine and entering Crown Road. The Ascent must be completed before the ordered League encounters. Shortcuts on Routes 21–24 require their field abilities; Route 25 requires the Champion flag. Dungeon inner chambers have a shortcut that opens after their objective.

The current data is a playable content scaffold, not a complete implementation of every design detail. Several side quests use explicit collection flags or species/type checks, while others remain simplified. The early species pools are hand-selected and remaining species are distributed across reserve routes rather than fully curated habitats. The terrain layouts reuse deterministic patterns with varied obstacles, paths and dressing, and the dungeon mechanisms are ordered switches rather than distinct physical simulations. The runtime must support complete opposing teams, legendary captures, individual reward items, and persistent quest state for the content to behave as intended. `tests/world.test.ts` checks map geometry, reciprocal links, simulated progression, and species coverage; it does not establish campaign balance or substitute for a playthrough.
